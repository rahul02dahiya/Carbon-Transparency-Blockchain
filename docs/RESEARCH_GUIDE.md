# Researcher’s Guide: Tools & Search Strategies

## Trusted Academic Platforms
To find high-impact PDFs (2022–2026), prioritize these journals identified as the most influential for this field:
1. **IEEE Access** (Leading journal for blockchain-based supply chains).
2. **International Journal of Information Management** (Top-tier impact factor).
3. **Sustainability** (Focuses on green technology and carbon-free strategies).
4. **Journal of Cleaner Production** (Foundation for sustainable supply chain theory).
5. **research.google** (Awesome for researchers providing [dataset search tool](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/) and other [research tools & resources](https://research.google/resources/)

## Pro Search Tricks
- **Narrow the Topic:** Avoid broad titles like "Blockchain Pollution." Use specific goals like 
  "Blockchain-based Life Cycle Assessment for Carbon Transparency".
- **Check Data Feasibility:** Before coding, ensure finding or simulate a labeled dataset. 
  Research without proper data becomes "weak".
- **Literature Review (PRISMA):** Use a systematic review approach to identify "research gaps" 
  in existing studies.
- **Citation Management:** Use tools like Zotero or Mendeley to handle and sort materials efficiently.
